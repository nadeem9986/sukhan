About SUKHAN

Sukhan — an Urdu word for poetry, for words chosen with care. We think a fragrance should work the same way: a few honest notes, arranged with intention, nothing wasted.

SUKHAN is a small, independent fragrance house. We compose in small batches and sell directly, one conversation at a time.
